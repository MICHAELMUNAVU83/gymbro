# GymBro Project Overview

## What the system does

GymBro is a fitness coaching platform for two types of users: athletes and trainers.

For athletes, the system helps them:
- sign up and choose their role
- complete onboarding with body stats and training goals
- receive an AI-generated workout program based on their profile
- view upcoming workouts and daily training targets
- log workout sessions, sets, reps, weight, and rest time
- track body weight, check-in photos, and personal progress over time

For trainers, the system helps them:
- set up a trainer profile
- invite clients into the platform
- manage their client list
- view client training activity and progress
- update or override exercises inside a client's plan
- monitor live workout sessions and review analytics across clients

## Core system flow

1. A user joins the app and selects either `athlete` or `trainer`.
2. Athletes complete onboarding, including body stats, goals, and preferences.
3. GymBro generates a structured training plan using AI.
4. Athletes follow the plan and log workouts inside the app.
5. Trainers can invite clients, review their progress, and adjust training where needed.

## Main features

- Role-based authentication and access control
- Athlete and trainer onboarding flows
- AI workout plan generation
- Workout program and exercise management
- Live workout logging
- Body stats and progress tracking
- Trainer dashboard and client analytics
- Client invitation and coach-client linking

## Product goal

The goal of GymBro is to give athletes a simple way to get a personalized training plan and stay consistent, while giving trainers tools to coach clients, monitor progress, and make better training decisions in one system.
